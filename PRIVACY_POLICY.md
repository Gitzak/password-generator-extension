# Privacy Policy

Password Generator respects your privacy.

## Data Collection
This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Local Processing
All password generation happens locally in your browser. No generated passwords are sent to any server.

## Permissions
This extension does not request unnecessary permissions and does not track browsing activity.

## Third-Party Sharing
No user data is sold, transferred, or shared with third parties.

## Changes
If this privacy policy changes in the future, updates will be reflected on this page.
